# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Lets people belong to one or more family groups so that a press reaches exactly those who share a group with the presser. Membership is managed centrally on the server and delivered to decorations without anyone touching them.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Family groups
A family group SHALL have an opaque id generated by the server and a display name of 1–40 characters. Group names SHALL NOT need to be unique. A device SHALL be a member of zero or more groups, and a group SHALL have any number of member devices.

#### Scenario: Grandchild in both grandparents' groups
- **WHEN** Emma's device is a member of "Brander Grandparents" and of "Smith Grandparents"
- **THEN** her device takes part in both groups at the same time

#### Scenario: Two families choose the same group name
- **WHEN** two unrelated families each create a group named "Grandparents"
- **THEN** they are two separate groups with different ids, and neither receives the other's presses

### Requirement: Membership supplied by the server
A device SHALL learn its group memberships (ids and names) and its firmware update channel from the server, through the broker. It SHALL do this when it connects and whenever they change, and SHALL apply changes without a restart or any action at the device. The device SHALL remember its last-known membership across reboots and use it until the server's current membership arrives. The device SHALL NOT offer any way to edit group membership on the device.

#### Scenario: Adding a member remotely
- **WHEN** the administrator adds Emma's device to "Smith Grandparents" while it is online
- **THEN** within about 10 seconds her device sends its presses to, and plays presses from, "Smith Grandparents", with nobody touching the device

#### Scenario: Removing a member remotely
- **WHEN** the administrator removes a device from a group while it is online
- **THEN** within about 10 seconds that device stops receiving presses from that group and stops sending its presses to it

#### Scenario: Reboot while the broker is unreachable
- **WHEN** a device in two groups reboots and cannot reach the broker
- **THEN** its settings page still lists its two last-known groups

### Requirement: Presses reach all of the presser's groups
A short press SHALL reach the devices of every member of every group the presser belongs to (see `thinking-of-you-messaging`).

#### Scenario: Emma thinks of both sides of the family
- **WHEN** Emma, a member of "Brander Grandparents" and "Smith Grandparents", presses her button
- **THEN** every other online device in either group plays Emma's song and pattern

### Requirement: Isolation between groups
A device SHALL receive presses only from groups it is a member of. The broker SHALL deny any device both subscribing to and publishing into groups it is not a member of. A device SHALL also discard any press that arrives on the topic of a group it is not a member of.

#### Scenario: Other side of the family
- **WHEN** a member of "Smith Grandparents" presses, and Grandpa Brander is only in "Brander Grandparents"
- **THEN** Grandpa Brander's device does not play anything

#### Scenario: Friend's family
- **WHEN** a member of a friend's family group presses
- **THEN** no device outside that group plays anything

#### Scenario: Attempt to listen to another group
- **WHEN** a device subscribes to the press topic of a group it is not a member of
- **THEN** the broker delivers no messages from that group to the device

### Requirement: Shared groups play once
When the presser and a receiver share more than one group, the receiver SHALL play the press once.

#### Scenario: Parent and child in both groups
- **WHEN** William and Emma are both members of both grandparents' groups, and William presses
- **THEN** Emma's device plays William's song and pattern once

### Requirement: Device in no groups
A device that is a member of no group SHALL show "not in any family group yet" on its settings page. A short press SHALL show the send-failed feedback. The device SHALL still report presence and receive firmware updates.

#### Scenario: Newly registered device
- **WHEN** a device has been registered on the server but not yet added to any group, and its owner short-presses
- **THEN** nothing is sent, the device shows the send-failed feedback, and its settings page explains that it is not in any family group yet

### Requirement: Administration through the server
The server SHALL provide admin pages, available only after logging in as the administrator, for these actions:

| Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| Devices | Register a device by its device id with a label; change a device's label and update channel; remove a device |
| Groups | Create, rename and delete groups; add and remove member devices |

When a device is registered, the server SHALL generate its broker password and show it only once. Removing a device SHALL revoke its broker access straight away. Changes SHALL take effect on the broker and on devices within about 10 seconds, without restarting the broker.

#### Scenario: Registering a device
- **WHEN** the administrator registers device id `d-3c71bf4a2b10` with label "Gran (Brander)"
- **THEN** the server shows a newly generated broker password once, and the device can connect after that password is entered on it

#### Scenario: Removing a device
- **WHEN** the administrator removes a registered device
- **THEN** its current broker connection is closed, its next connection attempt is refused, and it no longer receives any group's presses

#### Scenario: Visitor without admin login
- **WHEN** someone who is not logged in as the administrator requests any admin page
- **THEN** they are sent to the login page and no device or group data is shown

### Requirement: Broker keeps working without the server
If the server is unavailable, the broker SHALL keep enforcing the most recent permissions, and devices SHALL keep connecting, sending and receiving within their existing groups. The server SHALL check the broker's permission state when it starts and at least every 10 minutes, and SHALL restore anything that differs from the server's records, including device logins, without re-provisioning devices.

#### Scenario: Server stopped
- **WHEN** the server is stopped while devices are online
- **THEN** devices keep sending and receiving presses within their groups, and only admin changes and new releases wait until the server is back

#### Scenario: Broker permissions lost or altered
- **WHEN** the broker's permission state has been altered or lost outside the server
- **THEN** within 10 minutes of the server running, every registered device can connect with its existing password and has exactly its recorded group permissions again
